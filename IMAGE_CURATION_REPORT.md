## 3. Discernment & Rejection Notes (Graded Component)

### ❌ Rejected AI Asset #1: Hyper-Neon 3D Ribbon Texture
- **Prompt Used**: *"3D glossy abstract ribbon explosion with hyper-neon cyan and pink reflections"*
- **Reason for Rejection**: 
  - **Visual Noise**: The harsh cyan lens flares and multi-colored glossy reflections created intense visual clutter.
  - **Brand Conflict**: Violated our Identity Kit's core rule: *"calm enough that your work is the loudest thing on the page"*. The bright 3D ribbons competed directly with the UI data instead of framing it smoothly.
- **Replacement Selected**: A minimal, soft-glowing dark obsidian texture with subtle cherry blossom pink ambient waves (`curated_hero_bg.png`).
